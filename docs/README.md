- [Keep an eye on many sessions](many-sessions.md): know which session needs you and get there in one key
- [Look without going in](look-without-going-in.md): a session's output, its diff and its conversation

- [Several accounts, and running out of quota](several-accounts.md): start under the account you want,

- [Drive sessions from scripts and other agents](scripting.md): the commands as building blocks, and
  one agent handing work to another
- [Usage, cost and limits](usage-and-cost.md): what your usage would cost, and how much of your
